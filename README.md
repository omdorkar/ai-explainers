# AI in your language

Weekly plain-language explainers on AI in Gujarati and Marathi, for shop owners, families and local businesses in Vadodara. By Om Dorkar.

Live site: https://omdorkar.github.io/ai-explainers/

- One post a week, published on the day it is sent. Dates are real: `tools/check_posts.js` refuses any date before the series started (30 October 2026) or in the future.
- Posts not yet sent stay drafts (`published: null`) and are not shown on the site.
- Plan: `docs/SERIES_PLAN.md`. Weekly steps: `docs/HOW_TO_PUBLISH.md`.

Related project: [Vadodara Access Map](https://omdorkar.github.io/vadodara-access-map/).
