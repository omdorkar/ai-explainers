# Series plan: 60 weeks

One post a week. A post goes live only on the day Om actually sends it, with that day's date.
The order can change; if a post moves, update its `week` and the "Next week" line of the post before it.

Posts marked **(Om writes)** need real things that have happened by then (questions people asked, what changed).
They are kept as outlines with prompts and cannot be published until Om fills them in.
